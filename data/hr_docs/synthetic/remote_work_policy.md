# Remote Work Policy

**Document:** Remote Work Policy  
**Effective Date:** 2023-09-01  
**Version:** 1.2  
**Owner:** Human Resources & IT Operations  

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Acme Corp recognizes that flexible work arrangements can increase productivity, improve employee wellbeing, and broaden the talent pool. This policy defines the conditions under which employees may work remotely on a regular or occasional basis.

This policy applies to all regular full-time and part-time employees. It does not apply to temporary employees, contractors, or roles designated as on-site only in the job description.

---

## 2. Eligibility for Remote Work

Remote work eligibility is determined jointly by the employee's manager and HR, based on the following criteria:

- The role's core responsibilities can be performed effectively outside the office without degrading service quality, collaboration, or client relationships.
- The employee has completed at least six months of continuous service and is not currently on a performance improvement plan (PIP).
- The employee's home workspace meets the minimum safety and ergonomic standards described in Section 4.
- The employee has reliable high-speed internet access (minimum 25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload).

Eligibility is reviewed annually and may be revoked if performance, attendance, or collaboration standards are not maintained.

---

## 3. Remote Work Arrangements

Three categories of remote work arrangement are recognized:

**Fully Remote:** The employee's primary work location is their home or an approved remote site. On-site presence may be required up to four times per quarter for team meetings, client visits, or other business needs. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level approval in addition to manager approval.

**Hybrid:** The employee splits time between the office and a remote location. The minimum on-site requirement is two days per week unless an alternative schedule is approved by the employee's manager and HR. Team agreements on which days are "anchor days" (everyone in-office) take precedence over individual hybrid schedules.

**Occasional Remote:** The employee works from home on an ad-hoc basis (no more than two days per week on average over a rolling 30-day period) without a formal remote work agreement. Occasional remote work requires only manager approval and does not require an HR application.

---

## 4. Home Office Requirements

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining a safe, functional, and secure workspace. Minimum requirements include:

- A dedicated workspace free from significant distractions during core hours (9 AM–3 PM local time).
- An ergonomic seating arrangement; the company will reimburse up to **$400** per calendar year for ergonomic equipment (chair, monitor stand, keyboard) upon submission of receipts through the Expense Reimbursement process.
- Reliable connectivity; the company will provide a **$50/month** internet stipend for employees on a formal fully-remote or hybrid arrangement, payable via payroll.
- All company-issued equipment must be used in compliance with the IT Security Policy.

---

## 5. Core Hours and Availability

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours (9 AM–3 PM in their primary time zone) via Slack, email, and video conferencing. Calendar availability must be kept up to date.

Employees are not expected to respond to messages or emails outside their normal working hours. Managers may not penalize employees for not responding outside core hours, except in documented on-call or emergency situations.

---

## 6. Equipment and Expenses

Acme Corp provides a laptop and necessary peripherals to all employees, regardless of work location. Employees may not use personal devices to access company systems unless approved through the BYOD program governed by IT Security Policy.

Additional equipment requests must be submitted through the IT helpdesk portal. Approved peripherals for remote work (external monitor, webcam, headset) are covered up to **$600** as a one-time new-hire remote setup allowance; requests beyond this limit require manager and Finance approval.

---

## 7. Policy Violations

Working remotely in violation of this policy (e.g., from an unapproved country, or without completing the required agreement) may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Employees considering working from a country other than their country of employment must contact HR at least 30 days in advance to assess tax and legal implications.
