# Code of Conduct

**Document:** Code of Conduct  
**Effective Date:** 2023-06-01  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Owner:** Human Resources & Legal  

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Acme Corp's Code of Conduct establishes the standards of professional, ethical, and legal behavior expected of every employee, officer, contractor, and board member ("covered persons"). Acting with integrity — toward colleagues, clients, partners, and the public — is a foundational expectation and a condition of employment.

This Code supplements, and does not replace, applicable laws and regulations. Where a specific policy (e.g., Anti-Harassment Policy, Insider Trading Policy) provides more detailed guidance, that policy governs. In the event of conflict, the more restrictive standard applies.

---

## 2. Workplace Respect and Inclusion

Acme Corp is committed to providing a work environment that is safe, inclusive, and free from discrimination, harassment, and retaliation.

Discrimination on the basis of race, color, religion, sex (including pregnancy, gender identity, and sexual orientation), national origin, age, disability, genetic information, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law is strictly prohibited.

Harassment — including sexual harassment, unwanted physical contact, offensive jokes, derogatory language, and hostile behavior — is prohibited whether in person, in writing, or via electronic communication. Employees who experience or witness harassment should report it immediately using the channels described in Section 5. All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially.

Acme Corp does not tolerate retaliation against any employee who makes a good-faith report of a potential violation or who participates in an investigation.

---

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose and avoid situations in which personal interests could conflict — or appear to conflict — with the interests of Acme Corp. Common conflict-of-interest scenarios include:

- Holding a financial interest in a supplier, competitor, or customer (excluding diversified mutual fund holdings).
- Supervising or making employment decisions about an immediate family member or romantic partner.
- Accepting gifts, meals, or entertainment from vendors or customers beyond the thresholds in Section 3.1.
- Engaging in outside employment or consulting that competes with Acme Corp or uses company resources.

Employees who identify a potential conflict of interest must disclose it in writing to their manager and the Legal department as soon as it is identified. Disclosure enables the company to assess and manage the conflict, not necessarily prohibit the activity.

### 3.1 Gifts and Entertainment
Employees may accept nominal gifts (branded merchandise, food items) valued at less than **$50** from vendors or clients. Gifts exceeding this value must be declined or reported to the Legal department. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (gift cards) in any amount are never permitted.

Business meals and entertainment are permissible when they serve a legitimate business purpose, are reasonable in value, and are not intended to influence a business decision. Meals exceeding **$150 per person** require manager approval and expense reporting.

---

## 4. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property

Employees have access to confidential information about Acme Corp, its clients, employees, and partners as a necessary part of their work. This information must not be disclosed outside the company without proper authorization and must be handled in compliance with the Data Classification Policy and applicable privacy laws.

All work product created by an employee during employment and within the scope of their role is the intellectual property of Acme Corp. Employees must promptly assign and disclose any inventions, software, or creative works developed using company resources or related to company business, as specified in the Invention Assignment Agreement signed at hire.

---

## 5. Reporting Violations

Employees are encouraged and expected to report suspected violations of this Code, applicable laws, or company policy. Reports may be made to:

- **Direct Manager:** For non-sensitive matters where the employee is comfortable doing so.
- **Human Resources:** hr@acmecorp.com or in person; appropriate for workplace conduct issues.
- **Legal Department:** legal@acmecorp.com; for potential legal or regulatory violations.
- **Ethics Hotline (anonymous):** Available 24/7 at 1-800-555-ETHIC and online at ethics.acmecorp.internal. Anonymous reports are accepted and protected from retaliation.

Acme Corp investigates all reports promptly. Investigators follow a consistent process: initial review, evidence gathering, interviews, determination, and, where a violation is found, appropriate remedial action up to and including termination of employment.

---

## 6. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this Code will be taken seriously and handled consistent with the nature and severity of the infraction. Disciplinary actions range from a written warning to termination of employment, and may include required training, demotion, or referral to law enforcement where criminal conduct is involved.

Ignorance of this Code is not an acceptable defense. All employees must complete Code of Conduct training within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter. Training records are maintained by HR.
