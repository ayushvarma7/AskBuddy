# Performance Review Policy

**Document:** Performance Review Policy  
**Effective Date:** 2023-01-01  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Owner:** Human Resources  

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Performance reviews at Acme Corp are designed to align individual contributions with company goals, provide structured feedback, recognize high performers, and identify employees who need additional support. This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period.

The review process is not a disciplinary mechanism. Disciplinary procedures are covered separately under the Employee Conduct and Corrective Action Policy.

---

## 2. Review Cycle

Acme Corp conducts a formal performance review cycle twice per year:

- **Mid-Year Review (July):** Covers performance from January 1 through June 30. Focuses on progress against goals, development opportunities, and any course corrections needed. Mid-year reviews do not result in compensation changes.
- **Annual Review (December):** Covers the full calendar year (or the employee's period of employment if less than 12 months). Annual reviews include a comprehensive assessment of competencies, goal achievement, and overall performance rating. Annual review outcomes inform compensation adjustments effective February 1 of the following year.

New employees hired after October 1 will participate in a simplified onboarding check-in in lieu of the December annual review, with their first full annual review in the following December cycle.

---

## 3. Performance Rating Scale

All employees receive one of five ratings in the annual review:

| Rating | Label | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all goals; a role model for peers |
| 4 | Exceeds Expectations | Regularly surpasses most goals with high quality |
| 3 | Meets Expectations | Achieves goals reliably; performs at the level expected for the role |
| 2 | Needs Improvement | Falls short of some goals; requires a development plan |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory | Consistently fails to meet goals; performance improvement plan required |

The company targets a distribution of approximately 10% Exceptional, 25% Exceeds, 50% Meets, 12% Needs Improvement, and 3% Unsatisfactory, though managers are not required to adhere to a forced distribution. Calibration sessions between managers and HR ensure consistency across teams.

---

## 4. Goal Setting and Documentation

Employees and managers collaboratively set goals at the beginning of each review period using the SMART framework (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound). Goals must be entered into the performance management system (perf.acmecorp.internal) within **30 days** of the start of each review period or within 30 days of hire for new employees.

Each employee should have between **3 and 6 goals**, covering both business outcomes and professional development. At least one goal should address a skill or competency the employee wishes to develop.

---

## 5. The Review Process

**Step 1 — Self-Assessment:** Employees complete a self-assessment in the performance management system, reflecting on goal achievement, strengths, and development areas. The self-assessment window is open for two weeks before the manager assessment begins.

**Step 2 — Manager Assessment:** Managers complete their written evaluation within two weeks of the self-assessment window closing. Managers must address each goal explicitly and provide specific, behaviorally anchored examples.

**Step 3 — Calibration:** Managers meet with their peers and HR to calibrate ratings and ensure consistent application of the rating scale across the team and department.

**Step 4 — Review Conversation:** Managers deliver feedback to employees in a dedicated one-on-one review conversation. This meeting must be scheduled and completed within two weeks of calibration. Feedback delivery training is available through the L&D portal.

**Step 5 — Employee Acknowledgement:** Employees electronically acknowledge having received their review in the performance management system. Acknowledgement does not imply agreement; employees may add written comments.

---

## 6. Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

Employees receiving a rating of 1 (Unsatisfactory) or 2 (Needs Improvement) for two consecutive review periods, or whose performance poses an immediate risk to the business, will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP).

A PIP must include: specific, measurable improvement targets; a timeline (typically 30–90 days); a check-in schedule; and the consequence if targets are not met. PIPs are developed jointly by the manager and HR. Employees may request a copy of the PIP framework and are encouraged to engage constructively in the process.

---

## 7. Compensation Adjustments

Annual review outcomes are one input into compensation decisions. Merit increases are determined based on: performance rating, position within the salary band, and overall company budget. The compensation cycle is managed separately by Total Rewards and HR; specific merit increase guidelines are communicated to managers before each annual review cycle.
