# Parental Leave Policy

**Document:** Parental Leave Policy  
**Effective Date:** 2024-03-01  
**Version:** 1.1  
**Owner:** Human Resources  

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

Acme Corp is committed to supporting employees through the significant life event of welcoming a new child. This policy provides paid and unpaid parental leave entitlements for the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.

This policy applies to all full-time employees who have completed at least 12 months of continuous service. Part-time employees who meet the 12-month service requirement are eligible for pro-rated paid leave. Temporary and contract employees are not eligible.

---

## 2. Leave Entitlements

### 2.1 Primary Caregiver Leave
The employee designated as the primary caregiver (regardless of gender or biological relationship to the child) is entitled to:

- **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave** commencing from the child's date of birth, adoption date, or foster placement date.
- An additional **4 weeks of unpaid leave** may be taken immediately following the paid period, for a maximum continuous leave of 20 weeks.
- After 20 weeks, employees wishing to extend may apply for an unpaid leave of absence of up to 12 additional weeks under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) if they qualify, or under Acme Corp's general Leave of Absence policy.

### 2.2 Secondary Caregiver Leave
The employee designated as the secondary caregiver is entitled to:

- **4 weeks of fully paid parental leave**, to be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event.
- Secondary caregiver leave may be taken all at once or in two blocks of no less than 2 weeks each, subject to manager approval.

### 2.3 Adoption and Foster Care
Employees adopting a child under 18 or receiving a foster placement receive the same entitlements as biological parents. For international adoptions requiring travel, an additional **5 business days of paid travel leave** is provided.

---

## 3. Pay Continuity During Leave

During the paid portion of parental leave, employees receive 100% of their base salary. Variable compensation (bonuses, commissions) is not included in parental leave pay.

Employees may voluntarily choose to use accrued PTO to supplement pay during unpaid periods of leave. Refer to the PTO Policy for rules on how PTO interacts with leave. Acme Corp will not compel employees to use PTO concurrently with paid parental leave.

State or government-administered paid family leave benefits, where applicable, will be coordinated with Acme Corp's pay such that the employee receives no more than 100% of their base salary from combined sources.

---

## 4. Benefits Continuation

Health, dental, vision, and life insurance benefits continue uninterrupted during all approved parental leave (both paid and unpaid portions up to 20 weeks). Employee contributions are collected upon return to work.

Employees expecting a new dependent must enroll the child in applicable benefit plans within **30 days** of the qualifying life event (birth, adoption, or placement). To add a dependent, employees must complete the enrollment process via the annual benefits enrollment process described in the Benefits Enrollment Policy — the same portal and documentation requirements apply for mid-year life event enrollments. Failure to enroll within 30 days results in the employee needing to wait until the next open enrollment period.

---

## 5. Return to Work

Employees returning from parental leave will return to their same position or, if that position no longer exists, an equivalent position with the same compensation and level. Managers must not alter the returning employee's compensation, title, or core responsibilities in a manner connected to the parental leave.

A return-to-work meeting with the employee's manager and HR is recommended within the first week of return. Employees may request a phased return (reduced hours for up to 4 weeks at full pay) for primary caregivers, subject to manager and HR approval.

---

## 6. Notification and Documentation

Employees should notify HR and their manager as early as possible and no later than **8 weeks before** the expected leave start date for planned events (scheduled birth, adoption). For unplanned events, notification should be provided as soon as reasonably practicable.

Required documentation includes medical certification of birth/due date, adoption agency confirmation letter, or foster placement documentation. All documentation is treated as confidential and stored in the employee's secure HR file.
