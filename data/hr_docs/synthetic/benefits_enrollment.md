# Benefits Enrollment Policy

**Document:** Benefits Enrollment Policy  
**Effective Date:** 2023-11-01  
**Version:** 1.3  
**Owner:** Human Resources — Total Rewards  

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy describes when and how employees of Acme Corp may enroll in, change, or waive company-sponsored benefit plans including health insurance, dental, vision, life insurance, disability insurance, the 401(k) retirement plan, and the Employee Assistance Program (EAP).

All regular full-time and part-time employees scheduled to work at least 20 hours per week are benefits-eligible. Benefits eligibility begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment.

---

## 2. Enrollment Periods

### 2.1 New Hire Enrollment
New employees have a **30-day window** from their benefits eligibility date to make elections across all available benefit plans. Elections made during this window are effective on the benefits eligibility date.

Employees who do not make elections within the 30-day new-hire window will be defaulted into the company-sponsored base medical plan at the employee-only tier and will be unable to change elections until the next annual open enrollment period, except in the case of a qualifying life event (see Section 2.3).

### 2.2 Annual Open Enrollment
Open enrollment takes place each year during the month of **October** for coverage effective January 1 of the following year. Employees will receive a personalized benefits statement, a summary of plan changes, and step-by-step enrollment instructions via email and the HR portal (hr.acmecorp.internal) at least two weeks before the window opens.

During open enrollment, employees may:
- Switch between health plan tiers (employee only, employee + spouse, employee + children, family).
- Add or remove covered dependents.
- Elect, change, or waive dental, vision, life, and disability coverage.
- Enroll in or change contribution amounts for the FSA (Flexible Spending Account) or HSA (Health Savings Account) where applicable.
- Update 401(k) contribution percentages (though 401(k) elections can be changed at any time, not just during open enrollment).

### 2.3 Qualifying Life Events (Mid-Year Changes)
Outside of open enrollment, employees may make benefit changes within **30 days** of a qualifying life event (QLE). Qualifying life events include:

- Marriage or domestic partnership registration
- Divorce or legal separation
- Birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child
- Death of a covered dependent
- Involuntary loss of other coverage (e.g., spouse loses job-based insurance)
- Change in employee's or spouse's employment status affecting eligibility

To initiate a mid-year change, employees must submit a QLE request in the HR portal along with supporting documentation (marriage certificate, birth certificate, etc.) within the 30-day window. Changes take effect on the date of the qualifying event, provided the request is submitted on time.

## 3. Available Benefit Plans

### 3.1 Medical Insurance
Acme Corp offers three medical plan tiers through its carrier network:
- **Base Plan:** High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) paired with a Health Savings Account (HSA). Company contributes $500/year for individual coverage, $1,000/year for family coverage to the HSA.
- **Standard Plan:** PPO with moderate deductible; in-network and out-of-network coverage.
- **Premium Plan:** PPO with low deductible and broad specialist access; higher employee premium contribution.

Employee premium contributions vary by plan tier and coverage level and are published annually in the benefits guide available on hr.acmecorp.internal.

### 3.2 Dental and Vision
Dental coverage includes two preventive visits per year at 100%, with coverage for basic and major restorative procedures subject to annual maximums. Orthodontic coverage is available on the Premium Dental plan.

Vision coverage includes one annual eye exam and an allowance toward frames or contact lenses per year.

### 3.3 Life and Disability Insurance
Company-paid basic life insurance of 1× annual salary is provided to all eligible employees at no cost. Employees may purchase supplemental life insurance for themselves, a spouse, or dependents during open enrollment or a qualifying life event.

Short-term disability (STD) insurance covers 60% of base salary for up to 12 weeks after a 7-day waiting period. Long-term disability (LTD) insurance covers 60% of base salary from week 13 through return to work or age 65. Both STD and LTD are company-paid.

### 3.4 401(k) Retirement Plan
Employees are eligible to contribute to the 401(k) plan immediately upon hire. Acme Corp matches **50% of employee contributions up to 6% of base salary** (effective maximum match of 3% of salary). The company match vests over a 3-year graded schedule: 33% after year 1, 66% after year 2, 100% after year 3.

---

## 4. Adding Dependents

To add a dependent (spouse, domestic partner, or child) to health, dental, or vision coverage, employees must provide documentation of the relationship:
- Spouse: copy of marriage certificate
- Domestic partner: signed Affidavit of Domestic Partnership
- Child (biological or legal): copy of birth certificate or adoption decree
- Foster child: court order or placement agreement

Dependent documentation must be uploaded to the HR portal. Dependents added without documentation within 30 days of the effective date will be removed from coverage until documentation is provided.

---

## 5. Premium Deductions and COBRA

Benefit premiums are deducted from the employee's paycheck on a pre-tax basis (Section 125 cafeteria plan) where permitted. Annual elections are irrevocable for the plan year except in the case of a qualifying life event.

Upon termination of employment, employees and covered dependents are eligible to continue health coverage under COBRA for up to 18 months (or longer in qualifying circumstances). COBRA election packets are mailed within 14 days of termination. Premium costs under COBRA are the full plan premium plus a 2% administrative fee.
