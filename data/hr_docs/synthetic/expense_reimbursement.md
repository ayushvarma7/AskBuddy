# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document:** Expense Reimbursement Policy  
**Effective Date:** 2023-04-01  
**Version:** 2.1  
**Owner:** Finance & Human Resources  

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs the reimbursement of legitimate business expenses incurred by Acme Corp employees in the course of performing their job duties. All employees, including full-time, part-time, and temporary staff, are eligible for reimbursement of approved expenses.

Reimbursable expenses must be (a) ordinary and necessary for business purposes, (b) pre-approved where required by this policy, (c) supported by an original receipt, and (d) submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred.

---

## 2. Pre-Approval Requirements

The following expense categories require pre-approval before the expense is incurred. Expenses submitted without the required pre-approval may be denied.

- **Travel (airfare, rail, hotel):** Any trip with an estimated total cost above $500 requires manager approval. International travel requires both manager and VP approval at least 14 days in advance.
- **Conference and training registration fees:** Fees above $300 require manager approval. Fees above $1,000 require manager plus HR approval.
- **Software or subscriptions:** All software purchases, regardless of amount, require IT and manager approval.
- **Client entertainment:** Meals and entertainment with clients require manager approval and must include a list of attendees and the business purpose.

---

## 3. Reimbursable Expense Categories and Limits

### 3.1 Travel
Economy-class airfare is reimbursed in full for pre-approved trips. Business class is permitted for international flights exceeding 8 hours and requires VP approval. Employees must book through the company's preferred travel portal (travel.acmecorp.internal) when possible; outside bookings are reimbursed at the lesser of actual cost or the portal price.

Hotel accommodations are reimbursed at actual cost up to **$200/night** in standard domestic markets and up to **$350/night** in high-cost markets (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo, Singapore). Exceptions require VP approval.

Ground transportation (taxi, rideshare, public transit) is reimbursed in full with receipts. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate.

### 3.2 Meals
Daily meal per diems for approved travel:

| Destination | Per Diem (USD/day) |
|---|---|
| Domestic (standard) | $60 |
| Domestic (high-cost) | $80 |
| International | $100 |

Receipts are required for individual meal expenses above $25. Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable unless part of an approved client entertainment event.

### 3.3 Home Office (Remote Work)
Remote employees on a formal arrangement may claim up to **$400/year** for ergonomic equipment and receive a **$50/month** internet stipend as described in the Remote Work Policy. These amounts are processed through payroll or via the standard expense form with receipts attached.

### 3.4 Professional Development
Books, online courses, and professional certifications are reimbursable up to **$1,500 per calendar year** per employee with manager approval. Amounts above $1,500 require HR and VP approval. Employees who voluntarily leave within 12 months of receiving reimbursement above $500 must repay a pro-rated portion.

---

## 4. Submission Process

All expense claims must be submitted via the Expense Portal (expenses.acmecorp.internal) within **60 days** of the expense date. Late submissions (60–90 days) require manager justification. Submissions older than 90 days will not be reimbursed.

Steps:
1. Log in to expenses.acmecorp.internal.
2. Create a new expense report and attach receipts (PDF or image, max 10 MB per file).
3. Categorize each line item and enter the business purpose.
4. Submit for manager approval; the system will route to Finance automatically.
5. Approved reimbursements are paid in the next available payroll cycle (bi-weekly).

---

## 5. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable under any circumstances:
- Personal entertainment, streaming subscriptions, or gym memberships (unless part of an approved wellness benefit).
- Traffic and parking fines.
- Lost, stolen, or damaged personal property.
- Expenses related to commuting to an employee's regular place of work.
- Expenses incurred during an employee's personal time appended to a business trip beyond the applicable per diem period.
- Gifts to employees (covered by a separate recognition budget managed by HR).
