## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes the framework for Paid Time Off (PTO) accrual, usage, and carryover for all full-time and part-time employees of Acme Corp. Temporary, contract, and intern employees are not covered by this policy unless explicitly stated in their offer letter.

PTO encompasses vacation days, personal days, and sick leave in a single unified bank. Employees are responsible for managing their PTO balance to cover all unscheduled absences.

---

## 2. PTO Accrual Rates

Accrual is based on years of continuous employment and employment classification. PTO accrues on a per-pay-period basis beginning on the first day of employment, but is not available for use until the 90-day probationary period has been completed.

| Years of Service | Full-Time (hours/year) | Part-Time (pro-rated) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 80 hours (10 days) | Pro-rated by FTE % |
| 2–5 years | 120 hours (15 days) | Pro-rated by FTE % |
| 5–10 years | 160 hours (20 days) | Pro-rated by FTE % |
| 10+ years | 200 hours (25 days) | Pro-rated by FTE % |

Accrual is calculated as total annual entitlement divided by 26 pay periods. Employees who join mid-year receive a pro-rated entitlement for the remainder of the calendar year.

---

## 3. Carryover and Payout Rules

Unused PTO may be carried over from one calendar year to the next, subject to a maximum carryover cap. As of January 1, 2024, the carryover cap is **80 hours** for full-time employees and pro-rated for part-time employees.

Any PTO balance exceeding the carryover cap at December 31 will be forfeited. Employees are encouraged to plan their time off proactively and will receive automated balance reminders in October and November of each year.

Upon termination or resignation, accrued PTO that has not been used will be paid out at the employee's current base hourly rate, subject to applicable state law. In states where PTO payout on termination is not legally mandated, payout is still provided as a company policy benefit.

---

## 4. Requesting PTO

Employees must submit PTO requests through the HR portal (hr.acmecorp.internal) at least **two business days** in advance for absences of 1–3 days, and at least **ten business days** in advance for absences of four or more consecutive days.

Manager approval is required for all PTO requests. Managers must respond to requests within two business days. In the event of a conflict, the manager should consider operational needs alongside employee tenure and the order in which requests were received.

Unplanned absences (e.g., sudden illness) must be reported to the employee's manager before the start of the work day or within one hour of the scheduled start time. Follow-up documentation may be required for absences exceeding three consecutive days.

---

## 5. Interaction with Other Leave Programs

PTO runs concurrently with state-mandated sick leave where applicable. Employees on an approved leave of absence (medical, parental, or other) do not accrue PTO during unpaid leave periods.

For parental leave, employees may voluntarily use available PTO to supplement any unpaid portion of their parental leave entitlement. See the Parental Leave Policy for details on how PTO interacts with parental leave pay continuity.
